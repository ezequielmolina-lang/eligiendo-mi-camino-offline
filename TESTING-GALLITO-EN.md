# Try Gallito — the vocational coach that runs **offline**

**Open it here:** https://ezequielmolina-lang.github.io/eligiendo-mi-camino-offline/

Gallito is a career-guidance assistant that runs **inside your browser**, on your own computer. After the
first time it works **without internet**, and **nothing the student types leaves the device** (private by design).

> Tried it before today? Do a **hard reload** (Ctrl + Shift + R) when you open it, so you get the new
> version (it now runs the best model, *Trained on Opus*).

## What you need
- **Up-to-date Chrome or Edge**, on a laptop, PC, or Chromebook. *(Safari/Firefox not yet; phones may not work.)*
- **First time only:** internet and a little patience — it downloads the model (**~1.2 GB**) once and stores it.
  Use **wifi**.

## How to test it (about 5 minutes)
1. Open the link.
2. Enter a name and grade (or use the sample) and go to **Paso 1: Autoconocimiento** (Step 1: Self-knowledge).
3. The **first time** you'll see a download bar (~1.2 GB). Wait for it to finish — it can take several minutes
   depending on your connection. **This happens only once**; after that it runs with no connection.
4. Chat with Gallito as a 5th-year secondary student would: answer its questions about you, your interests,
   your doubts.
5. When you want, click **"Terminar y completar paso →"** (Finish and complete step) to move on.

## If something goes wrong

**Stuck on "Downloading / Loading"**
- It's the one-time ~1.2 GB download. Give it a few minutes on a good connection.
- If it really hangs: **Ctrl + Shift + R** (hard reload). Your progress is **not** lost (it's saved on the
  device) and the download resumes where it left off.

**Gallito keeps "thinking" (the "…") for a long time in the chat**
- The **first** reply after downloading can take a while (~30–60 s: the model warms up).
- If it doesn't answer: send a short message ("sigamos" / "let's continue") or click **"Terminar y completar
  paso →"**. Reloading (Ctrl + Shift + R) restarts the chat **without** losing your progress.

**Gallito repeats itself or goes in circles (a loop)**
- It's a small model; it sometimes repeats. Send it something **short and different** ("let's change topic",
  "please summarize") or move to the next step.
- For the test, **note which step it happened on** — that's very useful to us.

**It says something odd or invents a fact**
- It can happen (it's small). If it invents an institute, a salary, or a scholarship, **don't take it as
  true** — note it for the report.

## Important — safety
Gallito is a **research prototype**, not a counselor or an emergency service. If a student expresses that they
are in danger or feeling very bad, **talk to a trusted adult**, and in Peru call the **113 line (option 5)** —
free, any time.
